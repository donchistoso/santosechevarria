## The strategic call

The posting's own emphasis maps almost exactly onto what Santos has:

| Posting asks for | Santos has |
|---|---|
| "seamless transitions for customers from vehicle selection to the financing process" | 28 years supporting a 15-person sales floor, trained on hand-off |
| "secure financing approvals through established lender relationships" | 33 years of bank, captive, and credit-union relationships |
| "present... in a clear and compelling manner" | 6× Award of Excellence; Platinum in Security Plus since 2005 |
| "compliance with all lending requirements" | TILA, Red Flags, OFAC across thousands of funded deals |
| "high-performance, luxury environment" | ⚠️ **the gap** — volume Nissan/Honda, no luxury brand experience |

The letter **names the luxury gap in the second paragraph** rather than hoping
nobody notices. A hiring manager at a Ferrari store will spot it in ten
seconds; naming it first is the only version where Santos controls the framing.
The pivot is that lenders, structure, compliance, and hand-off don't change
with the badge — and that his numbers were graded annually by a manufacturer.

Coral Gables is also a genuinely short move from his Brickell Honda years and
his current Miami base — worth a line, since dealership managers care about
whether a hire will actually stay.

Dear Hiring Manager,

I have spent thirty-three years in South Florida finance offices — the last
twenty-eight at Bill Seidle Nissan Auto Group, and eight before that at
Brickell Honda, a short drive from your showroom. I am writing to apply for
the Finance and Insurance Manager position at THE COLLECTION.

Let me be direct about the one thing my résumé will not show you: I built my
career in high-volume franchise stores, not in luxury. What I bring instead is
three decades of the part of this job that does not change when the badge on
the hood changes — and a performance record the manufacturer graded every
single year.

- **Lender relationships that close difficult deals.** Thirty-three years of
  working relationships with banks, captives, and credit unions across the
  full credit spectrum. When a deal is tight, that network is the difference
  between a delivery and a customer who walks.

- **Product presentation that holds up under scrutiny.** Six-time Nissan Award
  of Excellence winner, and Platinum level in Nissan Security Plus products
  every year since 2005 — two decades of a manufacturer grading my penetration
  and my per-vehicle numbers.

- **A clean hand-off from the sales floor.** I supported a fifteen-person sales
  team and trained them on deal structure, disclosure, and how to bring a
  client into the finance office without losing momentum or trust. Your
  posting puts that transition at the center of the role. It is the part of
  the job I have spent the most years building.

- **Compliance without exceptions.** Truth in Lending, Red Flags Rule, OFAC —
  thousands of funded deals, and a clean record across all of them.

- **No learning curve on your systems.** CDK, DealerTrack, and Reynolds &
  Reynolds, every day, for years.

A client buying a McLaren deserves the same finance office a client buying a
sedan deserves: prepared, straightforward, and fast enough to respect their
time. I have delivered that in this market, to this city's customers, for more
than three decades, and I would like to do it for a group that expects the
standard to be higher.

I would welcome the chance to sit down and walk you through my numbers. You
can reach me at (786) 554-0173.

Sincerely,

**Santos Echevarria**

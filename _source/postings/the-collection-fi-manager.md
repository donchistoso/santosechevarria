## Summary
Pivotal role in the sales process: offering financing options, securing
approvals, presenting additional products and services. Ideal candidate has a
strong automotive financing background, exceptional customer service skills,
and the ability to work in a high-performance, luxury environment.

## Key responsibilities

### Finance & Insurance Operations
- Collaborate with sales to ensure seamless transitions from vehicle selection
  to financing.
- Present financing options, extended warranties, protection plans, and other
  F&I products clearly and compellingly.
- Secure financing approvals through established lender relationships —
  competitive terms, compliance with all lending requirements.
- Prepare all documentation for financing, leasing, and insurance; accuracy and
  adherence to legal and dealership standards.
- Calculate financing terms including taxes, interest rates, and fees.
- Build relationships with lending institutions, insurance providers, and
  partners to secure favorable terms.

### Customer Experience
- Deliver exceptional customer experience; clearly explain financial products
  and processes, address concerns, ensure satisfaction.
- Build and maintain trust — transparency and professionalism throughout.
- Provide ongoing support on financing, warranties, insurance inquiries.

### Compliance & Reporting
- Ensure compliance with federal, state, local law, including **Truth in
  Lending (TILA)** and the **Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA)**.
- Maintain accurate documentation of all deals, finance contracts, and
  aftermarket product agreements — **full audit readiness**.
- Conduct regular **internal reviews** of F&I processes to identify and correct
  non-compliant practices or documentation errors.
- Generate and submit timely **reports to dealership leadership and
  manufacturer partners** on F&I performance, product penetration, and
  regulatory compliance metrics.
- Oversee secure handling and storage of customer credit information and
  financial documentation — **data privacy protocols**.
- Coordinate with **external auditors or regulatory agencies** during
  inspections or compliance reviews.

## Qualifications
- ⚠️ **Minimum of 5 years of luxury automotive sales as an F&I Manager.**
- Comprehensive knowledge of automotive financing, leasing, and insurance
  products.
- Exceptional interpersonal and communication skills; ability to build rapport
  with **high-net-worth clientele**.
- Strong business acumen and understanding of the **luxury automotive market**.
- Strong attention to detail and organizational skills.
- Proven ability to meet and exceed sales targets and profitability goals.
- Proficiency in **CRM systems** and dealership management software.
- Valid driver's license and clean driving record.

## Working conditions
- Luxury showroom, plus off-site events and networking engagements.
- Flexibility for **evenings, weekends, and holidays**.
- Travel may be required for industry events, training, meetings.
- Closed Sundays.

## Benefits
Medical/dental/vision · supplemental (accident, critical illness) · 401(k) with
employer match · PTO · paid vacation · paid holidays · birthday off · closed
Sundays.

Equal Opportunity Employer.

# Goals — Santos Echevarria career initiative

Owner of the materials: **Santos Echevarria** (Juan's father-in-law).
Driver of this repo: **Juan Perez** (juan.perez@qpaynet.com).
Started: 2026-09-05.

## Situation
Santos is a career automotive Finance & Insurance manager — 33+ years, two
dealerships, currently at Bill Seidle Nissan Auto Group (1998–present). He is
looking to move to a new dealership. He has never had a resume built for a
modern hiring process, no cover letter, and no LinkedIn presence.

## Objectives
1. **Resume — two variants, both truthful and one page each.**
   - `resume-finance-manager.md` — lateral Finance Manager move. Primary.
   - `resume-fi-director.md` — F&I Director / F&I Manager-of-managers slant,
     leaning on the 15-person team, 33-year tenure, and award record.
2. **Cover letter — a real letter, plus a reusable template.**
   Dealership hiring is relationship- and numbers-driven; the letter should
   sound like a closer, not like a form.
3. **LinkedIn — a complete, findable profile.**
   Headline, About, two experience entries, skills, and the award record.
   Goal is recruiter-findable for "F&I Manager Miami" / "Finance Director
   automotive South Florida".
4. **Job search — a tracked pipeline.** Target dealership list and an
   application tracker so follow-up doesn't get lost.

## Ground rules
- **No fabricated numbers.** PVR, product penetration, CSI, deal volume, and
  gross figures stay in general language until Santos supplies real figures.
  This was an explicit instruction from the prior session and it holds.
- **Truthful reframing is fine.** Presenting existing facts (team size,
  tenure, awards, systems) in leadership language is not fabrication.
- **Private repo.** Contains personal contact info and, later, compensation
  targets and recruiter contacts.
- **One page** for both resume variants. Dealership GMs skim.

## Definition of done
- [ ] Both resume variants drafted, reviewed by Santos, exported to PDF + DOCX
- [ ] Cover letter template + one tailored letter for a real posting
- [ ] LinkedIn profile live with all copy from `linkedin/profile.md`
- [ ] Target dealership list built; tracker in use
- [ ] Real performance metrics collected and folded into both variants

# Which résumé to send

Three versions. Same facts in all three — 30 years, same employers, same dates,
same awards, no invented numbers. What changes is emphasis and vocabulary.

| File | Use it for | What makes it different |
|---|---|---|
| **`..._Resume_FinanceManager_General.pdf`** | **The default.** Any Finance Manager / F&I Manager opening at a volume or mainstream store. Use this unless there's a reason not to. | Straight chronological résumé in formal third person. Broad "Core Skills" list. This is the one Santos already approved in an earlier draft. |
| **`..._Resume_FinanceManager_LuxuryStores.pdf`** | High-line and luxury dealerships — The Collection, Braman, Prestige, any Porsche/Audi/Ferrari-type group. | Retitled "Finance & Insurance Manager." Bullets reordered to follow a luxury posting's own responsibility list. Says "clients" not "customers." Names "entry models to high-line units." Keyword-dense "Core Competencies." |
| **`..._Resume_FIDirector.pdf`** | Only where the posting is genuinely a **director / department-head** role. | Rewritten toward department ownership — F&I product strategy, lender portfolio, compliance ownership. **Read the caveat below before using it.** |

## Caveat on the Director version

A director title normally implies managing *other finance managers*. Santos's
record shows a 15-person **sales** team and a single-desk finance office. Until
we can confirm he supervised or trained other F&I managers, this version is
best used for **F&I Director at a single-point store** — where the director *is*
the finance office — not a multi-store or group role.

If it turns out he did supervise other finance managers, tell Claude: that
becomes the headline bullet and this version gets much stronger.

## Rule of thumb
Volume store → **General.** Luxury store → **LuxuryStores.** Department-head
posting at one store → **FIDirector.**

Every version is one page. All are rebuilt from `_build/` — if a fact changes,
all three get updated together.

# Photos

Source and edited images for the LinkedIn profile and any printed materials.

## Naming
- `santos-headshot-original-<YYYY-MM>.jpg` — untouched source, never edit in place
- `santos-headshot-linkedin-<YYYY-MM>.jpg` — square crop for the LinkedIn photo
- `santos-banner-<YYYY-MM>.jpg` — 1584×396 LinkedIn background, if used

## LinkedIn photo specs
- Square, 400×400 minimum; 800×800 or larger is better
- Face should fill roughly 60% of the frame — head and shoulders, not full body
- Under 8 MB, JPG or PNG

## Editing
Originals stay untouched. Edits are made to a copy and the recipe recorded
here so a crop can be redone or adjusted later.

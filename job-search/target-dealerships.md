To be built with Santos — he knows this market better than any list we could
research. Sitting down and having him name stores is worth more than an hour
of searching.

## How to prioritize
1. **Stores where he knows someone.** A GM, GSM, or lender rep who can walk
   his resume in. This is how dealership hiring actually works; treat it as
   the primary channel and the job boards as backup.
2. **Volume Nissan/Honda stores.** His award record and product numbers are
   most legible to the brands he's performed in.
3. **Large groups** (multi-store) — more openings, more movement, and a
   plausible path to a director role.
4. **Anything within his commute tolerance.** ⚠️ Need to confirm what that is.

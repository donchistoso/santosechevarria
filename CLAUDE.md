# Working in this repo

Career-materials initiative for **Santos Echevarria** (Juan's father-in-law),
an automotive Finance & Insurance manager in Miami.

## Read first
`BRIEF.md`, then `GOALS.md`, then `_source/facts-of-record.md`.

## Hard rules
1. **Never fabricate a performance figure.** No PVR, product penetration
   percentage, CSI score, deal count, or gross profit number goes into any
   deliverable unless it appears in `_source/facts-of-record.md`. Santos
   explicitly asked for general language instead of invented specifics.
   Truthful *reframing* of confirmed facts is fine; inventing data is not.
2. **Facts of record is the single source of truth.** Anything in a resume,
   cover letter, or LinkedIn field must trace back to it. New information from
   Santos goes into that file first, then into deliverables.
3. **One page** per resume variant.
4. **Edit sources, not exports.** `exports/` is generated output.
5. **Keep the repo private** — personal contact info, later comp targets.

## Working with Juan
Be concise. Lead with the outcome. Detail goes in the repo, not in chat.

## Open questions
`_source/facts-of-record.md` ends with a numbered list of open questions for
Santos. Check it before asking anything new — and add to it rather than
guessing.
